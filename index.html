How to stay on track

  • Payday first: debts and savings go out the day you're paid. What's left is your spending money.
  • Debit only. Leave the RBC card at home until it's paid off.
  • Log every purchase the same day. It's on your Today checklist.
  • Over in one category? Take it from eating out or weekends, never from a debt payment.
  • Extra money (the $765 co-op refund, tax refund, a 3rd paycheque month) goes to debt first.
  • Sunday review: check "Left to spend" and adjust the rest of the pay period.
  • Car waits until you're debt-free, and plan for winter rent before January.
